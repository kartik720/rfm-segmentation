RFM Customer Segmentation

Interactive Recency / Frequency / Monetary distributions by customer segment — Olist Brazilian E-Commerce dataset (93,358 customers)

Key insight: Big Spenders + At Risk customers make up ~56% of the customer base but drive ~83% of total revenue — retention strategy should follow revenue concentration, not customer count.
